Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What is Sustainers NEST?

A youth-led sustainability community focused on turning environmental awareness into practical action through four projects.

How large is the community?

The current documented figures are 25+ institutes reached and 300+ volunteers.

What are the four projects?

Seeds of Change Artists, Planting For The Future, Chasing Green and Trash To Treasure.

How can I volunteer?

Use the volunteer page to express interest. The production form should be connected to Sustainers NEST’s official volunteer process.

Can institutes or organizations collaborate?

Yes. The partnership page is designed for institute activities, project collaborations, expertise and relevant support.
