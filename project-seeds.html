PROJECT 01

Seeds of Change Artists

Empowering the next generation of environmental advocates by blending local climate education with creative expression.

THE OBJECTIVE

Climate learning becomes a creative voice.

Seeds of Change Artists is a grassroots educational initiative for primary-level young learners aged 5 to 12. It introduces young learners to environmental challenges affecting their own communities through accessible and interactive learning, then helps them grow into young advocates through creative expression.

After learning about local climate issues, young advocates observe their neighborhoods and translate what they notice into art. This helps build a personal connection to environmental challenges and gives young advocates a way to express what they see.

OUR APPROACH

Education meets observation, then observation becomes creative expression.

Learn. Observe. Create. Speak.

01

Learn locally

Young advocates explore climate and environmental issues connected to everyday life.

02

Observe closely

They look at their own surroundings for pollution, habitat changes and other challenges.

03

Create freely

Young advocates turn their observations into drawings and visual stories.

04

Find a voice

The artwork gives young advocates a first platform for environmental advocacy.
