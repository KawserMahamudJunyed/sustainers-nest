Trash To Treasure at Nabinagar Government Primary School

A two-part institute activity connecting environmental awareness with hands-on creative upcycling.

What happened

Learning first. Creating next.

Young innovators learned about responsible waste management, waste segregation and the 3Rs - Reduce, Reuse and Recycle - and then applied those ideas through creative upcycling.

Using discarded household materials, young innovators explored how everyday waste can become useful or decorative items through teamwork and creative problem-solving.

This activity is part of the wider Trash To Treasure initiative, which has reached 450+ young learners.
