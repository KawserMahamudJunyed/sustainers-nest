PROJECT 03

Chasing Green

Bridging the gap between climate awareness and actionable solutions through education and visual advocacy.

THE CORE IDEA

Move from climate awareness to practical action.

Understanding the climate crisis matters, but knowing how to respond is what makes that knowledge empowering. Chasing Green breaks down pressing environmental issues and connects them directly to practical, real-world solutions.

The program is designed to help young advocates see climate action at different levels, from global solutions to everyday citizen choices and the steps they can take right now.

OUR APPROACH

The workshop combines climate solutions, young advocate agency and creative advocacy so learning can travel beyond the classroom.

Understand. Choose. Create. Advocate.

01

Understand the issue

Young advocates explore pressing environmental problems and the solutions already being developed around the world.

02

Find the action level

The discussion looks at what global systems can do, what everyday citizens can do and what young advocates can do themselves.

03

Turn learning into visuals

Young advocates synthesize what they have learned into environmental awareness posters and creative advocacy messages.

04

Share the message

Visual advocacy gives young advocates a practical way to carry sustainability messages from the classroom into the wider community.
